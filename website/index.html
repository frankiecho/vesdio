VESDIO

Valuing Ecosystem Services Dependencies with Input-Output modeling.

An experimental desktop tool for scenario ("what if") analysis of how ecosystem services disruption — water stress, pollination decline, and more — propagates through global supply chains to affect production in your business sector, region, or investment portfolio. Built to support TNFD-style nature-dependency disclosures with a concrete, quantitative starting point.

Features

A guided three-step wizard on the left, rich results on the right.

The wizard

1

Position

Tell VESDIO who you are: a single asset (a region + sector, e.g. car manufacturing in Germany) or a diversified portfolio of multiple region/sector assets weighted up to 100%.

2

Shock

Define what happens: an ecosystem-service shock (e.g. water stress, pollination decline) mapped via ENCORE to affected sectors, a specific sector-region shock, or a custom multi-shock scenario.

3

Configure

Choose the reference year and the simulation method — Leontief (demand-side) or Ghosh (supply-side) — then run the simulation.

Result views

Summary

Before/after output bars and a waterfall chart showing which upstream regions and sectors drove the change.

Geographic Impact

A map view of where the impact on your position is concentrated globally.

Supply Chain Flow

A Sankey diagram tracing how the shock flows through intermediate sectors and regions to reach you.

Global Impacts

A sortable table ranking every affected sector-region by relative or absolute output change.

Historical Context

Compares your scenario's magnitude against historical production trends for the affected sector, where data is available.

Portfolio Breakdown

In portfolio mode, a per-asset breakdown plus the aggregated, weighted impact on the whole portfolio.

Scenario Builder & portfolios

✎

Scenario Builder

How it works

EXIOBASE global input-output data, ENCORE materiality mapping, and a standard multi-regional input-output shock model.

1. Data EXIOBASE 3, a global Multi-Regional Environmentally Extended Supply-Use/Input-Output database covering 44 countries, 5 rest-of-world regions, and 163 sectors.
2. Materiality ENCORE (Global Canopy & UNEP) links economic sectors to their ecosystem-service dependencies; VESDIO uses ENCORE's "High"/"Very High" dependency ratings to decide which EXIOBASE sectors are shocked by an ecosystem-service scenario.
3. Shock A user-chosen percentage magnitude is converted into an absolute production change (relative to 2021 reference-year values) for the affected sector-region(s).
4. Propagation The change propagates across all regions and sectors using either a Leontief demand-side model or a Ghosh supply-side model, then is aggregated back to a relative percentage change for your position or portfolio.

Full methodology, data-source citations, and formulas are documented in the repository README.

Assumptions & limitations

VESDIO is highly experimental and its methods are not empirically or scientifically validated via peer review. Treat every result as a rough, directional indicator — not a number to put in a disclosure filing unmodified.

  • Does not use scientific pressure–response coefficients to translate an ecosystem-service disruption into a sectoral production change — the magnitude you choose is applied directly as a production shock.
  • Does not use the more rigorous (but computationally expensive) shock-simulation methods, such as interior-point optimization; results could be inaccurate as a result.
  • Sectors ENCORE rates "Moderate", "Low", or "Very Low" dependency are assumed unaffected by first-order effects of an ecosystem-service shock (though they can still be hit by second- or higher-order supply-chain effects).
  • Has no "materiality" thresholds — it does not tell you whether a dependency is financially material to your business; you still need to interpret the output in your own business context.
  • Is based on 2021 EXIOBASE data and is not forward-looking: results should be read as "what if this shock had happened in 2021?", not as a projection of a future shock (and do not reflect events like COVID-19).
  • Cannot replace in-depth scenario analysis incorporating your business's specific operating context and supply-chain characteristics.
  • Was built with extensive AI-assistance (see the AI-use declaration in the footer) — review its outputs and code with appropriate caution.

VESDIO is not (yet) endorsed by, or part of the recommended toolkit for, TNFD disclosures.
